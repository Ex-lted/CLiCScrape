# CLiCScrape Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026

## 1. Introduction

CLiCScrape is a browser extension designed to help Multimedia University (MMU) students retrieve available course and class information from the MMU CLiC website and automatically generate personalized class schedules.

This Privacy Policy explains how CLiCScrape accesses, uses, and stores information when you use the Extension.

## 2. Information We Collect

CLiCScrape does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable student information.

The Extension does not access or store:

- Student names or student IDs.
- Login credentials, passwords, or authentication details.
- Personal contact information.
- Other personal information unrelated to course scheduling.

CLiCScrape accesses course-related information available through the MMU CLiC website, such as course names, available classes, and class times, solely to provide its scheduling functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by CLiCScrape is used exclusively to provide its core features, including:

- Retrieving available courses and class times from the MMU CLiC website.
- Organizing course availability information.
- Generating and displaying class schedules.
- Saving relevant course data and scheduling preferences locally in the browser, where applicable.

The Extension does not use this information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated commercial purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All information processed and stored by CLiCScrape remains within the user's browser.

The Extension does not transmit course information, generated schedules, or user data to external servers operated by the developer. No backend service is used to collect or store this information.

Users can clear locally stored Extension data through available Extension controls, where provided, or by clearing the Extension's associated browser storage.

## 5. Website Permissions

CLiCScrape requires access to the MMU CLiC website to retrieve course availability information needed to generate class schedules.

The Extension uses browser storage permissions, where applicable, to save course data and scheduling information locally.

These permissions are used solely to support the Extension's intended functionality. CLiCScrape does not use these permissions to access student identities, login credentials, or unrelated personal information.

Users remain subject to MMU CLiC's own terms and policies when accessing that website.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

CLiCScrape does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

The Extension does not send stored course data or generated schedules to external analytics services, advertising networks, or developer-operated servers.

Course information retrieved from the MMU CLiC website is used only within the user's browser to provide scheduling functionality.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Course data, generated schedules, and relevant preferences may remain in the browser's storage to support the Extension's functionality.

Users can delete locally stored information through available Extension controls, where provided, or by clearing the Extension's associated browser storage.

CLiCScrape does not maintain a separate external database of user information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

CLiCScrape is intended for university students and individuals who need assistance organizing course schedules. The Extension does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Extension's functionality or data practices change. Any revised version will be published with an updated effective date.
